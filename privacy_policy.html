Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gallery Cleaner ("we", "us", or "our"), identified by Application ID com.iusmansial.photocleaner, treats user privacy and data access when you use our mobile application.

1. Local Device Media Access (Photos & Videos)

Gallery Cleaner is a local, gesture-first media organization and photo cleaning application. The app requires access to your device's photo and video gallery (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) solely to display your photos and videos locally within the app and allow you to swipe, organize, and delete duplicate or unwanted media.

Your media never leaves your device. We do not collect, transmit, upload, or backup any of your photos, videos, or metadata to any external server or cloud service. All processing is strictly local on your mobile device.

2. Data Collection & Advertising

We do not collect personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number). However, our application integrates third-party services for advertising:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): We display non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and coarse location data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

For more information on how Google processes advertising data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Deletion & User Control

Media deletion is strictly user-driven. Photos and videos queued for deletion are only removed from your device when you explicitly confirm the action via the OS deletion dialog. We have no access to restore or backup deleted media once confirmed by you.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.
